Slider

A horizontal value control with configurable minimum, maximum and starting values.

Declaration

solSlider slider;
solSlider_Init(&slider, "Volume", 50, 160, 200, 24,
               0.0f, 100.0f, 25.0f);
solSlider_SetCallback(&slider, OnSliderChanged);
solUI_AddElement(&slider.Element);
