Button

A clickable control with normal, hover and pressed states and optional callbacks.

Declaration

solButton button;
solButton_Init(&button, "Click Me", 50, 50, 160, 40);
solButton_SetCallback(&button, OnButtonClicked);
solUI_AddElement(&button.Element);

Members

MemberDescription
ElementCommon SolarUI element data.
LabelText displayed on the button.
StyleBackground, hover, pressed, border and text colours.
StateCurrent button state.
OnClickCallback invoked when clicked.
